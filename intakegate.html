IntakeGate AP intake under control

Invoice extraction treated as untrusted input. Extracted fields pass through nine deterministic controls — arithmetic tie-outs, schema completeness, vendor master match, PO format, period and duplicate checks — before anything is allowed to post. Clean documents flow straight through; anything that fails a control is held with the reason attached.
