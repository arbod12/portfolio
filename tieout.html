TieOut bank reconciliation engine

Matches a bank statement against the general ledger in three passes: exact match, then date-window match, then fuzzy proposals on description similarity. Every fuzzy proposal is a hypothesis that must survive an amount and date test before it clears — a suggestion never becomes a match on its own authority. Ends with a reconciliation that proves out to the penny.

Ledgers

CSV: date,description,amount. Deposits positive, payments negative.
Pass 3 uses description similarity to propose pairings the first two passes missed. A proposal is accepted only if the amounts agree within tolerance and the dates fall inside the window; otherwise it is logged as rejected with the reason. The similarity score ranks candidates — it never clears one.
