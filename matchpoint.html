MatchPoint three-way match engine

An invoice can be internally perfect and still be wrong. MatchPoint matches invoice against purchase order against receiving report, line by line, and tests the three questions arithmetic alone cannot answer: was it ordered, was it received, and was it billed at the agreed price? Over-billing, short shipments, unauthorised substitutions and quantity creep surface as priced exceptions.

Documents

Three ledgers, CSV. Columns: doc_id,line,item,description,qty,unit_price. Receipts use qty received; price is ignored there.
Matching is deterministic and priced. Every exception carries the dollar impact of paying it as billed, which is what turns a discrepancy list into a recovery number.
