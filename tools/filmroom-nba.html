Positions — names and jobs

PG — 1Point guard

Brings the ball up and runs the offense.

SG — 2Shooting guard

Usually the best outside shooter; works off screens.

SF — 3Small forward

A wing who can shoot, drive and defend several positions.

PF — 4Power forward

Plays inside and out; a “stretch 4” shoots threes.

C — 5Center

Usually the tallest: protects the rim, rebounds, sets screens.

Ball handler · screenerPick-and-roll roles

The screener blocks the ball handler’s defender, then rolls to the rim or pops out to shoot.

Strong side · help sideSides of the floor

Strong side has the ball; help-side defenders sag toward the paint.

CloseoutRunning at a shooter

Sprint, then chop your steps so you can contest without flying by.

Box outRebounding

Put your body between your man and the rim before going for the ball.

BLOB · SLOBInbound plays

Baseline and sideline out-of-bounds plays. After a late timeout the ball can be advanced to the frontcourt, 28 ft from the baseline.
